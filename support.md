# Second Brain — Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to **stanislavkuzka@gmail.com**. Please mention your device (iPhone, iPad or Mac) and its system version.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do my tasks get to my other devices?**
Second Brain syncs through your own private iCloud database. Sign in with the same Apple ID on each device and keep iCloud turned on for Second Brain. New items usually arrive within a minute; bringing the app to the front starts a catch-up.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your devices and in your private iCloud database. The developer cannot read it. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

**Does voice capture send my speech anywhere?**
No. Speech is transcribed on your device. Download the voice language in the app when it asks.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete items in the app; deletions sync to your other devices. To remove everything, delete the app on each device and remove its data in your iCloud storage settings.

**Which languages does it understand?**
Dates, times and reminders in English, Ukrainian, Russian, German, Spanish, Romanian and French.
